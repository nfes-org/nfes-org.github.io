#### Mathias Horstmann (SPWLA Director, Europe) is encouraging geoscientists to participate in the International Student Paper Contest: **"Let’s keep the European flags up!"**

Participating in the SPWLA International Student Paper Contest is an excellent opportunity to showcase your research on a global stage, gain valuable feedback from experts in the field, and network with professionals and peers. The contest is open to undergraduate, master's, and Ph.D. students, offering a platform to highlight innovative work in petrophysics, geosciences, and related disciplines.

#### Why participate?

- Share your research with a global audience of industry experts and academics.
- Enhance your professional profile and visibility within the geoscience community.
- Compete for recognition and awards at the prestigious SPWLA Annual Symposium, which will be held in Dubai this year.

#### Travel support for current NFES members and students from Norway who want to become members
Last year, [NFES provided grants to help our new member from the University of Bergen attend the contest](/2024/12/03/student-grants-delivered-for-2024.html). 
We are pleased to announce that [you can apply this year to partially cover travel costs](/2024/05/10/student-travel-grant.html) if your paper is accepted. 
Take advantage of this opportunity to represent your university and Norway and compete with others in Dubai!

#### Deadline

All submissions must be completed **by February 1, 2025**. Don't miss this chance to advance your career and contribute to the global geoscience dialogue.

#### How to submit?

For more details and submission guidelines, visit the (SPWLA International Student Paper Contest page)[https://www.spwla.org/Symposium/Symposium/International-Student-Paper-Contest/International-Student-Paper-Contest.aspx].

Let's represent Norway's geoscience in this year's contest, too! Good luck to all participants!
