At the March 5 NFES meeting, **Charlie August** will present **CCUS from emitter to storage – where are we and what do we need**.
 
### Abstract
Building a new industry from scratch is a complex task, especially when it involves a diverse set of parties, policies, and companies. However, CCUS is crucial for meeting our net zero goals and ensuring safety and security for future generations. Even with years of subsurface CO2 injection experience, the ambitious decarbonisation goals of nations and companies depend on the rapid development of new commercial models and technical solutions across the entire CCUS value chain. Balancing market forces and policies against technical and economic risks, we will discuss what is needed for projects to succeed, the main risks involved, and who is currently leading the way in Europe and beyond.
 
### Bio
**Charlie** is Regions Business Development Leader for CCUS at **Baker Hughes**, based in Stavanger. He has a master’s degree in engineering from Bath in the UK, and has held roles in product development, commercial management, and project management over the last 15 years in the energy sector. He now leads the business development team for CCUS, looking across the full value chain from emitter to storage, and how CO2 technologies and solutions can be applied to create economic and technical success for projects across the globe.
