The  Annual General Assembly (Årsmøte) 2025 of the Norwegian Formation Evaluation Society (NFES) that will be held in public on Wednesday, 4th of June 2025 at 10:00 am (Norwegian time) prior to the technical meeting in person in the Solastranden Gård.

 

### NFES Årsmøte (General Assembly) 2025 - Agenda
- Approval annual & financial report 2024.
- Relief past NFES board (2024/25) and reconfirm for 2025-2026/27
- Membership status report
- Technical meetings & one-day seminar
- NFES and NORCE Geosteering workshop
- Sponsorship status – how can we achieve the required budget
- Field trip plans
- Future IT systems, LinkedIn, and web presence/ support
- Student support budget 2025/2026 (Norway)
- AOB
 

All NFES members are cordially **welcome to participate** in order to ensure all decisions are made based on our members and society interest.

**Make your Vote Count:** if an election is required for individual board roles/positions a ballot will be conducted with all active members of the society during the NFES General Assembly (Årsmøte) 2025 held June 4th.
