## About the event

**The 4th Stavanger Geosteering Workshop Keeps its Local Focus and Global Perspective**.

Due to the high cost of offshore operations in a harsh environment, 
the North Sea is in the top league in developing cutting-edge technologies for formation evaluation and geosteering. 
Many innovative solutions pioneered for the North Sea are now commercially available worldwide. 
Extensive research, technology development, and investments in innovation remain the key factors that provide more value for each well delivered in the region, keeping it competitive worldwide. 
With the increased focus on the green shift, the traditional hydrocarbon projects are joined by wells, which enable the energy industry in a broader sense.
Our precious workshops in 2019, 2022, and 2024 each gathered almost 100 experts and got excellent feedback.
Motivated by the series' success, NFES and NORCE invite you again to the Oil Capital of Norway (Stavanger) to meet other geosteering experts and share ideas to drive the energy industry forward in the following decades.

## Call for Abstracts

NORCE and NFES invite abstracts presenting new ideas, technical advances, and practical experiences in geosteering and related formation evaluation for the **4th Geosteering and Formation Evaluation Workshop**.

**Abstract submission deadline: 31 January 2027**
<!-- Internal note: we can extend the deadline to 1 February 2027 if needed. -->

We welcome methodological contributions and field case studies that improve subsurface understanding, make better use of available data and support timely steering decisions.

Topics include, but are not limited to:

* **Measurements and modelling.** Advances in logging and modelling for ultra-deep azimuthal resistivity (UDAR) and emerging sensing technologies.
* **Geosteering without UDAR.** Methods and field applications using conventional logging measurements and drilling data, supported by geological models.
* **Multiphysics integration.** Fusion of electromagnetic, seismic and other measurements to support formation evaluation, look-ahead imaging, and post-well analysis.
* **Uncertainty and decision-making.** Uncertainty assessment, well-placement optimization, and real-time decision support.
* **AI and automation.** Data-driven methods, generative modeling, automated workflows, and expert advisory systems.
* **Field experience.** Operational challenges, lessons learned, multidisciplinary collaboration, and training.
* **Applications beyond petroleum.** Geosteering for geothermal energy, tunnelling and other subsurface operations.

Contributions addressing other advances relevant to geosteering are equally welcome.

### Abstract guidelines

Please follow the SPWLA-style format outlined below:

1. **Title and authors** - provide the abstract title and each author's full name, affiliation and email address.
2. **Abstract text** - submit 300-600 words, organised into four sections of 75-150 words each:
   - *Introduction* - define the problem and its relevance.
   - *Procedure* - describe the methods and highlight what is new.
   - *Results / Observations* - present the main findings and technical contributions.
   - *Conclusion* - summarise lessons learned, practical implications and potential applications.
3. **Supporting figure** - submit one figure or graph illustrating the methodology or key results (without company logos or author names). For easiest interpretation, mention it in the abstract text.
4. **Relevant references** - if you are extending or summarising published work of others or of your own within your abstract, add references **[1]** (optional, but encouraged).

Note that **abstracts must be technical and non-commercial**. Do not include trade names or promotional content.
