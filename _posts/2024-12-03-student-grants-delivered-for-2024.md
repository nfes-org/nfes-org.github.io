#### NFES continues to stimulate Norwegian students by offering Travel Grants. 

During the [3rd Geosteering and Formation Evaluation Workshop](/2024/03/26/geosteering-workshop-2024.html), **Hibat Errahmen Djecta and Yasaman Cheraghi** (both from the University of Stavanger) received a grant for their presentation about [automated geosteering using reinforcement learning](/2024/09/25/018-improvement-workshop-abstract.html). 

Dler Mirza also presented a travel grant to **Durra Handri Saputera** from the University of Bergen for the past SPWLA Symposium in Rio. Durra is on a research visit at UT Austin, and his primary supervisor, Prof. Morten Jakobsen, accepted the grant on Durra's behalf.
