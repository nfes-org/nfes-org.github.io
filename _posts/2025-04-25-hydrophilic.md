The technical presentation **"Prototype HLT Pilot Testing Offshore Norway 2024"** will be given by **Trond Rolfsvåg from Hydrophilic**.

 

### Abstract

The Hydrophilic Logging Tool (HLT) introduces a novel measurement capability that enables the estimation of the Oil-Water Contact (OWC) without the need for drilling through it.  

 
The prototype HLT was tested twice offshore Norway in 2024. This presentation will discuss the insights gained from both tests, including the tool failures encountered during the first pilot and the successful demonstration of water pressure measurement within an oil reservoir during the second pilot. Special emphasis will be placed on HLT measurements in reservoirs containing perched water.

 

### Bio

**Trond Rolfsvåg** is a playful Reservoir Engineer and CEO of Hydrophilic with a Master’s Degree in Petroleum Technology from University of Stavanger (UiS). Worked for big integrated oil companies for 30 years. Started Hydrophilic in 2016 to develop new tools that can make the E&P industry even more efficient.
