The **Norwegian Formation Evaluation Society (NFES)** is evolving, and we need **your creativity** to design our **new logo**!

Since **1981**, NFES has been the Norwegian chapter of **SPWLA**, fostering knowledge sharing in **formation evaluation**, supporting research, and connecting professionals from oil companies, service providers, universities, and research institutes.

Unlike NFES's proud history, the **exact origins and age of our current logo are unknown**! Now is your opportunity to contribute to a **new chapter in NFES history**.



### 🏆 How to Participate

Submit your **original logo design** by **October 13, 2025** to [**vp_pr@nfes.org**](mailto:vp_pr@nfes.org?subject=NFES%20Logo%20Submission).

Submissions are welcome in **any image format**—we expect to collaborate with a **graphic designer** to develop the final version as professional vector graphics.



### ✏️ Design Guidelines

- Inspired by formation evaluation, well logging, and geoscience  
- Clean, modern, and recognizable at any scale  
- Suitable for both digital and print use (website, presentations, merchandise)  
- Acknowledging NFES's Norwegian roots is a plus!
- Original work only

**Design notes**
- ⚠️ Creative use of AI tools is allowed, but any AI assistance must be clearly acknowledged in your submission.
- ⚠️ Participants may submit multiple distinct hand-crafted designs, but only one AI-generated design per person is allowed.


### 🗳️ How the Winner is Selected

- All submitted designs will be part of a **public vote** within the NFES community  
- The **final logo will be selected from among the top-voted entries**, in collaboration with a designer  

- ⚠️ *Note: The top-voted design may not be used exactly as-is, but it will play a central role in shaping the final logo.*


### 🎉 What's in it for You?

#### 🏅 Winner:

- Official **recognition** on NFES platforms  
- **NFES membership fee covered for one year**, giving you **free access to all technical meetings**  
- The **prestige** of shaping NFES's future visual identity!

#### 🥈 Top 3 Finalists:

- **Free invitation** to attend a technical meeting of your choice (including lunch) during the year  
- Public recognition and a **special mention** on NFES channels


[**Now's your chance—submit your design and leave your mark on NFES!**](mailto:vp_pr@nfes.org?subject=NFES%20Logo%20Submission)
